House Rules

7 chapters · 29 rules · last edited 2 Aug

Earning
Chapter 1 · 5 rules
1.1

Every task carries an XP value from 5 to 30, set by difficulty. Finish before the deadline and the full value is awarded.

1.2

Finish every task in a domain group on time for a +10 XP bundle bonus. If any task in it ran late, the bonus is +7.

1.3

XP lands the moment a kid taps Complete — not on approval. You're notified and can verify afterward.

1.4

Personal Hygiene tasks are tracked as streaks, not points. 19 of the 150 library tasks score zero XP by design.

1.5

Only admins can assign tasks, approve completions, request proof, and approve allowance.

Deadlines
Chapter 2 · 5 rules
2.1

Daily tasks close at 7:00 PM household time. Weekly tasks close Sunday 7:00 PM, monthly on the last Sunday at 7:00 PM.

2.2

Poppins sends task notifications, including a final nudge 30 minutes before anything is due.

2.3

Late Credit is applied the instant Complete is tapped, and never recomputed. A late task still keeps the streak and carries a late indicator.

5 → 310 → 715 → 12 20 → 1625 → 2030 → 25
2.4

At 11:59 PM anything unfinished becomes Expired and can no longer be completed.

2.5

An expired weekly or monthly task costs its XP but never breaks a streak.

Streaks
Chapter 3 · 4 rules
3.1

Only daily and weekday tasks feed the streak.

3.2

A streak ends after 3 misses in a row, or 3 misses inside any rolling 7 days.

3.3

Streak Rescue costs 10% of that week's XP after one missed day and 20% after two consecutive. A third consecutive miss loses the streak permanently. Decline and the XP stays, the streak goes.

3.4

Recess switches tasks off and freezes streaks at their current value — leave on 12 days, return on 12 days.

Crowns
Chapter 4 · 6 rules
4.1

The Week's Crown goes to the highest XP each week. Monthly Sovereign is settled on the last Sunday.

4.2

First place is gold, second silver, third bronze.

4.3

Ties produce duplicate ranks — two firsts, then two seconds.

4.4

The winner's sheet shows tasks completed, on-time count and late tasks. Every member's sheet is viewable.

4.5

Champion's Record keeps every crown won, by member.

4.6

Trophies unlock on lifetime XP. The top tier, Most Glorious, sits at 100,000 XP.

Rewards
Chapter 5 · 5 rules
5.1

The household runs one of five models: XP only, Allowance, XP + Rewards, XP + Allowance, or the full system.

5.2

Rewards are tied to finishing that day's assigned tasks and homework. Each reward carries its own frequency.

5.3

A reward is either instant or requires approval, set per reward.

5.4

Allowance is recorded in ChoreMaxx, not transferred — no money moves through the app. Approvals follow the household schedule, and Approve now always works.

5.5

Mint Reward creates one of your own, on any reward page.

Proof
Chapter 6 · 2 rules
6.1

On chores, admins can request proof on a completed task. It's optional and per task — it verifies the work, it doesn't gate the reward.

6.2

Homework keeps its own tab, and photo proof is required. It's configured per child, on by default, and an admin confirms it.

Household
Chapter 7 · 2 rules
7.1

Two roles: Admin and Helper. Only admins hold the permissions in 1.5.

7.2

Admins can change any rule here. The kid version rewrites itself to match, in their words.

Deadlines run on household-local time. Changes take effect the next day, so nothing already in progress changes value mid-task.

House Rules

Earning

How much is a task worth?

Between 5 and 30 XP, set by difficulty when you assign it. On-time completion pays the full value.

What's the bundle bonus?

+10 XP for finishing every task in a domain group on time. If one of them ran late, it's +7.

When does XP actually land?

The moment your kid taps Complete — not when you approve. You're notified and can verify afterward.

Why did a task score zero?

Personal Hygiene tasks are tracked as streaks, not points. 19 of the 150 library tasks are zero-XP by design.

Who can assign and approve?

Admins only — assigning, approving completions, requesting proof and approving allowance. Helpers do the work and see their own record.

Deadlines

When is everything due?

Daily tasks at 7:00 PM household time. Weekly close Sunday 7:00 PM, monthly on the last Sunday at 7:00 PM.

Does anyone get warned?

Poppins sends a final nudge 30 minutes before anything is due, alongside the usual task notifications.

They finished at 8 PM — what did they lose?

Late Credit applies: 5→3, 10→7, 15→12, 20→16, 25→20, 30→25. It's calculated the instant Complete is tapped and never recomputed.

The streak survives, and the task carries a late indicator.

Can they still do yesterday's task?

No. At 11:59 PM anything unfinished becomes Expired.

Does a missed weekly task break the streak?

No. It costs the XP, but only daily and weekday tasks can break a streak.

Streaks

What breaks a streak?

Three misses in a row, or three inside any rolling 7 days.

What does Streak Rescue cost?

10% of that week's XP after one missed day, 20% after two consecutive. A third consecutive miss loses it permanently. Decline and the XP stays but the streak ends.

What happens when we travel?

Turn on Recess. Tasks stop and streaks freeze where they are — leave on 12 days, come back on 12 days.

Crowns

How is the weekly winner decided?

Highest XP takes The Week's Crown — gold, then silver and bronze. Monthly Sovereign settles on the last Sunday.

What if two kids tie?

Ties produce duplicate ranks — two firsts, then two seconds. Nobody gets bumped down.

What's on the winner's sheet?

Tasks completed, on-time count and late tasks. Every member's sheet is viewable, not just the winner's.

Where do past wins live?

In Champion's Record, by member. Trophies unlock on lifetime XP and top out at Most Glorious, 100,000 XP.

Rewards & allowance

What can we reward with?

Five models: XP only, Allowance, XP + Rewards, XP + Allowance, or the full system. Rewards are tied to finishing that day's tasks and homework, each with its own frequency.

Does a reward need my approval?

Your choice, per reward — instant or requires approval. Mint Reward creates one of your own on any reward page.

Does ChoreMaxx send the money?

No. Allowance is recorded here, not transferred — nothing moves through the app. Approvals follow your schedule, and Approve now always works.

Proof & household

Can I check they actually did it?

Yes. Request proof on any completed chore — optional, per task. It verifies the work, it doesn't gate the reward.

How does homework proof work?

A photo is required, configured per child and on by default. An admin confirms it. Homework keeps its own tab beside Tasks.

What can a Helper do?

Complete their own tasks, see their record and their crowns. Everything in the admin list stays with admins.

Can I change these rules?

Any of them. The kid version rewrites itself to match, in their words.

Deadlines run on household-local time. Rule changes take effect the next day, so nothing already in progress changes value mid-task.
