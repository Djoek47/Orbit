Positions and scales use springs; opacity and colour use timings at the same feel. Nothing slides sideways, nothing bounces. Reduce Motion swaps every move for a cross-fade and keeps the timing.
ALWAYS One card at a time. Their words above it. A way out in every state. The count before it happens. 44 px targets, including a row's ×. Colour earned by meaning.
NEVER A spinner where a card could show what it has. A face on a grocery. Two cards for one sentence. A failure line over a finished act. A card inside a card. A personal accent repainting a domain.
