SLOT ORDER

The card is built in the order they said it

A slot the person filled is never asked for again, and the first empty slot is the one in focus. Whatever they said first sits at the top of the card — so the same sentence in a different order gives a different card, and both feel obvious.

“add the dishes… for Mia”
WHAT Dishes
WHO Mia
WHEN in focus
Only the day is asked for, with Today / Tomorrow ready.
“Mia should do the dishes”
WHO Mia
WHAT Dishes
WHEN in focus
Mia's face is already lit at the top. No picker opens.
“tomorrow, someone needs to do the dishes”
WHEN Tomorrow
WHAT Dishes
WHO in focus
Faces come up last, because that is the only gap left.
FILLED BY SPEECH Mint fill, no question. It is never re-asked, and a later guess from the model cannot overwrite it.
IN FOCUS The first empty slot, dashed, with its chips ready. Exactly one at a time.
STILL TO COME Dimmed and unlabelled until its turn, so the card never looks like a form.
