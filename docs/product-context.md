## Product Definition

Choremaxx is an AI-powered Household Operating System. It is not a chore app, calendar app, grocery list, smart-home app, productivity app, or chatbot. The product promise is one calm command center that helps a household answer:

- What needs to be done?
- Who is responsible?
- What is missing?
- What is happening today?
- Who is overloaded?
- What should we do next?

Core positioning: Choremaxx is the AI co-manager for modern households.

Primary tagline: The AI Operating System for Modern Households.

Best marketing line: Run your household together.

## Emotional Goal

Orbit should make users feel:

- Everything is under control.
- I know what matters today.
- I am not carrying this household alone.
- My family is working together.

Avoid stress, guilt, pressure, complexity, childish gamification, corporate SaaS energy, and generic productivity-tool patterns.

## Core Audience

- Household Manager: usually carrying invisible mental load. Needs visibility, relief, fairness, and fewer reminders.
- Supporting Parent or Adult: wants to help but needs clarity on what matters now.
- Child: needs simple responsibilities, motivation, recognition, XP, badges, and rewards.
- Single Parent: needs automation and a second-brain feeling from Nova.
- Roommates: need transparency, fairness, and shared accountability.
- Grandparent, caregiver, babysitter, cleaner, or guest: needs simple, limited, accessible temporary access.

## Primary Roles

- Owner: full control, billing, permissions, analytics, household deletion.
- Admin Parent: tasks, groceries, calendar, rewards, invites, mental-load analytics.
- Adult Member: completes and creates household work, receives notifications, accepts responsibilities.
- Child: sees assigned work, completes tasks, earns XP, unlocks badges, claims rewards, has restricted permissions.
- Guest: temporary limited access for assigned tasks and today schedule only.

## Core Modules

- Home Command Center: household momentum, tasks, groceries, events, Nova briefing, mental load balance, child progress, notifications, quick actions.
- Tasks: responsibilities with category, assignee, due date/time, XP, difficulty, repeat rule, completion status, proof requirement, mental-load value.
- Groceries: low/missing/purchased states, quick missing-item capture, store-aware future recommendations.
- Calendar: school, pickup/drop-off, homework, projects, activities, appointments, recurring routines, responsibilities.
- Rewards: XP, levels, badges, streaks, reward shop, parent approval, family goals.
- Nova: proactive household co-manager, daily and weekly briefings, recommendations, alerts, rebalancing, grocery intelligence.

## MVP Scope

MVP should prove that people want an AI household operating system:

- Account creation and sign-in.
- Household creation and joining.
- Invite members by code, link, or QR.
- Role system.
- Task assignment and completion.
- Grocery list with quick missing item flow.
- Basic calendar.
- XP and badges.
- Household Momentum score.
- Basic Nova briefing mockup.
- Smart notification placeholders.
- Shopping recommendation placeholder.

## Roadmap Stages

- Foundation: auth, household creation, invite flows, roles, database foundations, push reminders.
- MVP: core command center, tasks, groceries, calendar, rewards, Nova briefing, momentum, mental-load visibility.
- Growth: real Nova intelligence, richer notifications, advanced grocery intelligence, reports, location-aware suggestions.
- Platform: voice AI, smart home integrations, advanced analytics, Apple Watch/iPad/Vision Pro, broader household ecosystem.

## Success Metrics

- Household Momentum target: 85%+.
- Task completion target: 80%+.
- Weekly active households target: 70%+.
- Monthly retention target: 60%+.
- Nova engagement target: at least 1 interaction per household per day.

## Competitive Moat

Orbit should own the empty quadrant of household + active AI management. Moats:

- Mental Load Engine.
- Nova household context.
- Household Momentum score.
- Gamified family participation.
- Shopping intelligence.
- Family network effects.

Ultimate product-market fit signal: families say, "We can't imagine running our household without Orbit."
